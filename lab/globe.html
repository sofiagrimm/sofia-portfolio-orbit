Active
Neuroscience · Yale SOM New Haven, CT
Higley Lab · Locus Coeruleus
Characterizing locus coeruleus axon degeneration in PPT1-deficient mice to investigate norepinephrine dysfunction in Parkinson’s disease. Confocal microscopy, IHC, perfusion protocols.
History of Medicine · Yale New Haven, CT
Harvey Cushing Brain Tumor Registry
Archival research with Dr. Megann Licskai on Cushing’s brain tumor registry. I’ve transcribed all 420 case files and am building a cross-referenced record of the patients behind them. An abstract from this work is headed to the American Association for the History of Medicine meeting in 2027.
Biomedical Engineering · Yale SEAS New Haven, CT
PCDD · Activated-Carbon Screening
Biomedical Engineering Innovation Fellow on a patient-controlled drug deactivation device under Dr. Dan Wiznia. I sourced a 14-carbon panel from five suppliers and wrote the characterization and adsorption-screening protocols. I lead the HPLC work, validating HPLC and UV-Vis methods against the DEA non-retrievable standard.
Prior Research
Astrophysics · Fermilab Batavia, IL
Gravitational Lens CNN
Generated 40,000-image benchmark datasets of double-source plane gravitational lenses & Einstein rings. Trained PyTorch CNNs for automated morphological classification. Published at AGU Annual Meeting 2023.
Proteomics · Michigan State East Lansing, MI
Sun Lab · Top-Down Proteomics
Established the first solid-phase microextraction CZE-MS/MS system for top-down proteomics of mass-limited samples. It raised injection capacity about 30-fold over standard CZE-MS and resolved 0.05 mg/mL ubiquitin at an intensity of 3.8E6, where the standard method gave no detectable signal.
Genetics · Stanford Palo Alto, CA
Stan-X · Enhancer Trap Line
Developed an enhancer-trapped Drosophila line on the LexA-LexAop binary expression system, now deposited at the Bloomington Drosophila Stock Center. Ran the ligation and PCR protocols, dissected offspring brains to screen 40 lines by GFP fluorescence, and mapped the P element insertion to CG11050 by inverse PCR and FlyBase BLAST.
Medical Shadowing · Clínica Santa Isabel Sevilla, Spain
Clínica Santa Isabel · Medical Shadowing
Shadowed doctors at Clínica Santa Isabel in Sevilla, Spain, all in Spanish.
STEM Research · CCIR Cambridge, UK
Cambridge Centre for International Research
