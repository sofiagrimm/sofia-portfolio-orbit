Hi, I'm Sofia. I study brains, old case files, and the systems around them

I grew up in Hong Kong, moved to Chicago at fourteen, and now study Molecular Biophysics & Biochemistry at Yale, with a second major in Ethics, Politics & Economics.

Most days I'm imaging axons in the Higley Lab, screening carbons for a drug deactivation device, or reading Harvey Cushing's case files in the archive. The rest of the work is in the card catalog.
