origin story

Hi, I'm Sofia!

Born and raised in Hong Kong for my first fourteen years, then transplanted to Chicago for high school, I'm now a student at Yale University pursuing a B.S. in Molecular, Cellular & Developmental Biology and a B.A. in Ethics, Politics & Economics. I'm drawn to the places where science meets systems — a protein's folding behavior, a disease mechanism, a market for risk, a policy question about emerging technology.

My research has wandered happily across computational and wet labs: neurodegeneration, genetics, proteomics, astrophysics, machine learning, and mathematical optimization. Right now I'm at the Higley Lab at Yale School of Medicine, characterizing locus coeruleus axon degeneration in PPT1-deficient mice to investigate norepinephrine dysfunction in Parkinson's disease.

Off campus, I'm a Product & Strategy Associate at Adaptional (Y Combinator S25), where we're building claims intelligence for the insurance industry — including a 50-state, AI-queryable compliance library. Back at Yale Engineering, I lead business development for the PCDD, a patent-pending medication dispenser-deactivator.

Outside the lab I consult, edit, and occasionally win hackathons. I'm a Client Lead at the Yale Healthcare Consulting Collective, Director of Social Events on the Yale College Council, and an Online Editor at the Yale Undergraduate Law Journal.
